Glass Samples

All four keep today's glass exactly (same see-through, same colours), so text is as readable as it is now. Only depth changes: shadow, tone and the edge of the bars. Pick A, B, C or D at the top and look at each in light and dark. Scroll the small app, open the dialog, the menu and the toast.

1. The Layers

From the back to the front: the page, a card, the bar that content slides under, and a dialog.

A · Today. A single soft shadow on cards, and a big one on dialogs. The control to compare against.

B · Soft Lift. Cards get a three-part shadow (a tight edge, a mid and a wide soft one), so they float a little. The top bar throws a soft shadow once content has scrolled under it. Dialogs get a deeper shadow and a slightly darker scrim.

C · Tonal Layers. Depth by tone, as on a paper stack. The page is a step darker, cards are almost solid white, the nav is in between. Very light shadows. This is the most readable of the four. In dark, the page goes near black and each level is a little lighter.

D · Soft Edge. B's shadows, and content scrolls under a mostly solid bar that fades out softly below it, so you see the content slide away without any colour showing through the bar.
