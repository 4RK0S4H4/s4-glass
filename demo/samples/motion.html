Motion Samples

Picked: B, now built into the toolkit. This page is kept for comparison. Pick A, B or C at the top, then try each part below. All three stay inside the rules already settled: 150–250 ms, easing out, only what you touched moves, nothing blinks, and nothing waits for an animation before it takes the next key. With Reduce Motion on in your phone's settings, everything below is instant.

1. Feedback on Actions

Press the buttons and chips, flip the switches, tick the boxes, copy the amount, and archive to see the undo toast.

A · Today. Buttons dip 1px. The switch slides in 0.15 s. The tick pops in with a slight overshoot. Copy turns green for 0.6 s. The toast fades up and disappears all at once.

B · Apple Quiet. Buttons and chips shrink to 97% under your finger and settle back. The switch thumb stretches while you hold it, as on iOS. The tick draws in from left to right. Copy turns green for 0.6 s and the icon becomes a tick. The toast rises a little, then fades away.

C · Softer. The same as B with a deeper press (96%) and a slower settle at the end of each movement. It feels a bit more physical.

3. Switching Tabs and Chips

Switch between the tabs, then pick a month from the chip row. On a phone the row scrolls sideways.

A · Today. The pill slides in 0.24 s, and the new content fades up 6px.

B · Apple Quiet. The pill glides and slows down as it arrives. The new content fades in without moving. The chosen month scrolls itself to the middle of the row.

C · Softer. As B, plus the pill gives a little while you press it, and the new content comes into focus from a light blur, as in iOS.
