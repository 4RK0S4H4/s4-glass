Date Picker Samples

The browser's date picker is blue and unstyled, so S4 Glass draws its own: a calendar pop-over for dates and a month picker for "Sep 2026" boxes. On a desktop both options are identical. The options differ on a phone. Try the fields below, with the keyboard too (arrows, Page Up / Down, Home / End, Enter, Escape), and type a date such as 9/30/2026. Fictional data only.

A · Calendar Sheet on Phones. Every device gets the S4 calendar. On a phone it slides up as a bottom sheet with big 42px days, the same look and the same "Today" and "Clear" buttons as on desktop. One look everywhere, and the rules (earliest and latest allowed date) are enforced the same way. Cost: it is a little slower than the phone's wheel for a date far away, and we have to keep it working on every phone.

B · The Phone's Own Picker. Desktop gets the S4 calendar; on a touch screen the field turns into the phone's own date picker (the wheel on iPhone, the calendar sheet on Android), the same way the S4 dropdown steps aside on touch. Familiar, fast, free keyboard and accessibility support, and it respects the phone's language. Cost: it looks different from the rest of the app, and "Today" and "Clear" depend on the phone.

RecommendedB · The Phone's Own Picker. On a phone the job is "get to a date quickly with a thumb": the system wheel or calendar is already learned, handles any year in two flicks, and brings the right keyboard and screen-reader behaviour for free. It also matches what we already decided for the dropdown (S4 on desktop, native on touch), so the app has one rule for controls on a phone. Choose A only if the same look on every device matters more to you than speed and familiarity.
Recommendation basis: my own design judgement (the user's task on a phone, how far the date usually is from today, scan path and thumb reach, consistency with the dropdown decision, and accessibility). The front-end design skill (frontend-design) was not available in this session, so it was not used; please add it to the session.
