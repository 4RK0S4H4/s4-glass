Glass Samples

Four levels of see-through. A is today. B, C and D let the material (white in light, dark grey in dark) win, with only a faint tint from what is underneath. Shadows use the flat style in all four and are tried on the other page. Pick one at the top, check light and dark, scroll the small app so content passes under the bar, and open the dialog.

1. The Materials

Each tile sits over a busy stripe. The closer a tile is to solid, the less of the stripe you see through it.

A · Today. Glass at 62% (82% for dialogs). Today. On the light page the colour behind shows quite a lot.

B · Mostly Material. The material leads. Bars 90%, cards 82%, nav 80%, dialogs 96%, with the colour boost off. What is underneath is a soft tint, not a picture.

C · Nearly Solid. The most solid of the four. Bars 95%, cards 90%. Almost flat white (or dark grey); you only notice a trace, and the blur does the work.

D · Material + Fade. As B, with a soft fade under the bar so scrolled content melts away instead of cutting off at the bar.
