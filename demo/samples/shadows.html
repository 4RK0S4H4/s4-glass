Glass Samples

Four shadow styles on the same glass. A is today. B, C and D are flatter; menus, dialogs and toasts keep a shadow in all of them because they float over the page. Pick one at the top, check light and dark, scroll the small app, hover or press the number cards, and open the dialog and the menu.

1. The Cards

A page, a card, a tappable card (hover or press it), and a dialog.

A · Today. A soft shadow on every card and a big one on dialogs. The control to compare against.

B · Flat. No shadow at rest. Cards are separated by a hairline border and their fill only. Menus, dialogs and toasts still get a shadow because they float over content. Calmest, and the most flat.

C · Whisper. Cards get a barely-there 1px line of shadow, and the top bar gets a hairline shadow only once content has scrolled under it. Almost flat, with a hint of edge.

D · Lift on Touch. Flat at rest. Cards you can tap rise a little while hovered or pressed, so shadow only appears when it means something. Try the three number cards.

2. In an App

Scroll inside the frame. Open the dialog, the menu and the toast to see the depth over content.
