A · Today. There are two glass levels, 62% and 82% white in light and 4.5% white in dark, both with the same blur. The light page is flat grey, so in light mode the glass mostly reads as white.

B · Apple Ladder. There are three levels, as on iOS and macOS. Thin (55%) is for bars that content scrolls under. Regular (66%) is for cards and the nav. Thick (86%, more blur) is for dialogs and sheets. The light page gets a faint wash of the accent so the glass has something to frost. In dark, bars become a smoky dark glass rather than white.

C · Clearer. The same three levels, more see-through (36 / 48 / 72%), with more blur and a stronger colour wash behind. This is closer to the newest iOS look. It's the most glassy, but the third-level grey gets weaker over busy content.
