Density Samples

The same transactions page at different densities. Pick A, B, C or D at the top; where there is a density switch, it sits in the top bar of the small app. Watch how many rows fit without scrolling (the line under this text), and check it on your phone too: touch screens always keep tap targets at 40px or more, whatever the density.

A · One Density. Today's look only: calm and spacious. No setting. Simple, but data-heavy screens scroll more than they need to, and it can't suit both a first-time user and someone who reconciles all day.

B · Two Modes. Standard (today) and Compact, chosen by the user in a setting that is remembered. Compact tightens table rows, buttons, cards and the nav, and drops the base text a notch (14px to 13px). It's the usual answer for data-heavy tools.

C · Two Modes, Auto. As B, but the default follows the device: Compact on a wide screen with a mouse, Standard on touch and small screens. The user can still choose either. "Auto" is the setting's default, shown here as the first choice.

D · Three Steps. Compact, Standard and Roomy (larger text, 44px rows). The most flexible, and the most to design, test and explain. Roomy helps low-vision users and big touch screens.

RecommendedC · Two Modes, Auto. Your reconciliation tools are data-heavy and used at a desk, so compact on a wide screen with a mouse saves scrolling, while phones and touch screens stay comfortable; anyone can still override it.
Recommendation basis: S4 Glass rules and your earlier decisions. The front-end design skill was not available in this session, so it was not used.
