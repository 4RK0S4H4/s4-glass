Chart Samples

The same five charts in three colour schemes. Everything else is shared: thin marks, 2px gaps instead of borders, quiet grid, text always in text colours, a legend for two or more series, a tooltip on hover or focus, and a table view. Pick A, B or C at the top and check light and dark. Hover, or tab to the bars.

A · Accent First. Series 1 is your app's accent, so the charts look like the app. Then blue, aqua, yellow and violet, always in that order (the same hues and order as the validated reference set, with its magenta slot replaced by the accent). A single series is a soft tint of the accent, with the current mark solid. Up to five series.

B · Reference Set. The eight-hue validated set in its fixed order, blue first, whatever the app's accent is. The most distinguishable for many series, and the colours never clash with status colours. The charts look less like the app: a green app gets blue charts.

C · One Hue. The quietest: the accent, a lighter step of it, then greys. It follows the "one loud colour" rule most closely. Series differ only by lightness, so it leans on labels, the legend and the table view, and it holds at most four series.
