Month-End Status Samples

A month-end dashboard card for one company, in three situations: items still open and not flagged, bank and books agreeing outright, and books agreeing only because some items are flagged for next month. Today the last two look the same (green, 0.00). Fictional companies and amounts.

RecommendedB · Show the Bridge. The worry is that "0.00" hides money that hasn't hit the books yet. B answers it with the numbers themselves: the real bank-to-SAP gap, minus what's flagged for next month, equals what's left. A reviewer sees the size of the carry-over at a glance, and the same three lines read the same in every state, so the eye learns one place to look. A only changes a word, which is easy to miss when scanning several companies; C is the most complete, but listing items makes cards different heights and long on a phone.
Recommendation basis: the front-end design skill (used), applied within the S4 Glass rules.
