Navigation Samples

One account page, four ways to find your way around it. Pick A, B, C or D at the top, click around in the small app, and check it narrow too (on a phone, the side nav becomes a tab bar). The page header (title, subtitle, actions) is the same in all four; what changes is where you are, and how you switch between the views of one record.

A · Today. No trail, no tabs. The side nav goes to places; the segmented pill switches the list below. Fine for flat apps; with a detail page you have no way back except the nav.

B · Tabs + Back. A small "Accounts" back link above the title (one level up, which is also what a phone needs), and page tabs under the title for the views of this one account. The current tab is a neutral fill; the accent stays on the side nav. The segmented pill is kept for filtering the list.

C · Tabs + Trail. As B, but the full trail (Accounts / Northwind / Operating), so you can jump several levels. On a phone it collapses to the back link. Best for apps that go deeper than two levels.

D · Nested Nav. No tabs: the current section opens in the side nav and shows its views underneath. The header carries the trail. Keeps every view one click away and the header light, but it makes the side nav longer, and on a phone it falls back to tabs.

  • Nav goes to places. Tabs switch between peer views of one thing. Segmented filters or changes the mode of one list. Never use two of them for the same job.
