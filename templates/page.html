Rules that keep every screen calm

Decided once, on a real app, so no new project has to argue them again.

One loud colour

The accent fills the main action, the current place and what needs attention. Everything else stays neutral.

Dark is glass, not grey

A near-black page with a faint bloom of the accent; fills glow softly, text never turns accent-coloured.
